About

I hold an MSc in Medical Biotechnology from Edinburgh Napier University and a BTech in Biotechnology from Anna University. My research has covered A549 cell-based assays (WST-1 and IL-8 ELISA), nanoparticle work and whole-genome sequencing analysis, and my laboratory training includes HPLC, FTIR and GLP documentation.

For two years I was a duty manager at SSP Group in Edinburgh, running a team of 15+ in a time-critical, compliance-focused setting. That work gave me a habit of accurate records, clear escalation and meeting deadlines, which is what drug safety and clinical research work depends on.

I am now building my focus in pharmacovigilance, clinical research and clinical data management through structured training and practice. The skills section below separates what I have done from what I am still learning.
