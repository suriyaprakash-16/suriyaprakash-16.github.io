04 / RESEARCH

Research that shaped
my scientific approach.

2023—2024 MASTER’S DISSERTATION

Silver nanoparticles, nanocellulose & A549 lung epithelial cells

Investigated cell viability and inflammatory response in A549 cells exposed to silver nanoparticles (AgNPs) and nanocellulose (NC), individually and alongside LPS. Used WST-1 for cell viability and IL-8 ELISA for inflammatory response, with treatments performed in triplicate.

Methods A549 cell culture · WST-1 · IL-8 ELISA · GraphPad Prism
Reported observations Higher AgNP concentrations reduced viability and elevated IL-8; lower NC concentrations showed non-toxic/protective effects in the reported experimental context.
JAN—JUL 2024 RESEARCH SCHOLAR

Multi-omics and machine-learning-assisted anti-malarial research

Remote research collaboration with Gen'Omics Research Hub (Genomac Hub), supporting a programme focused on anti-malarial R&D and peptide-based drug discovery. Worked with WGS data from multidrug-resistant bacterial strains, including read trimming, genome assembly, contig extraction and biosynthetic gene-cluster analysis.

Methods & tools WGS workflows · Geneious · antiSMASH · machine-learning-assisted peptide prediction
Research direction Genomic surveillance and computational exploration of therapeutic peptide candidates.
2021—2022 UNDERGRADUATE PROJECT

Biosynthesised iron oxide nanoparticles & molecular docking

Explored plant-extract-mediated biosynthesis of iron oxide nanoparticles, physicochemical characterisation and pharmacological activity, alongside molecular docking studies investigating potential anti-cancer activity against hepatocellular carcinoma-related targets.

Methods UV spectroscopy · DLS · FTIR · pharmacological assays · molecular docking
